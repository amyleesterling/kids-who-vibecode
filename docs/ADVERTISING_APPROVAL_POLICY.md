# Advertising Approval Policy

## Absolute boundaries

No agent may spend money, activate a campaign, increase a budget, change targeting, connect an ad account, or transfer child/parent submission data into an advertising system without Amy’s explicit approval for that exact campaign. Database campaign drafts enforce `activation_allowed = 0` in this milestone.

Never target, retarget, profile, or build lookalikes from children, child submissions, parent contact collected for supervision, reviewer data, or family participation. Do not place third-party advertising or behavioral tracking on the child-facing service.

## Proposal-only scope

The Social and Growth Agent may research adult-facing placements, draft audience/context options, recommend a capped budget and timing, make preview assets, estimate outcomes with uncertainty, and prepare a campaign draft. Approved audience categories may include adults in parenting, education, libraries, camps, after-school learning, and community organizations—subject to current platform policy and legal review.

## Per-campaign approval packet

Amy’s future decision must identify:

- platform/account;
- objective and adult audience;
- exact creative, destination, and tracking parameters;
- budget cap, dates, geography, bid/optimization mode, and frequency limits;
- excluded audiences and confirmation that no child/family intake data is used;
- platform policy and privacy/legal review dates;
- measurement plan using aggregate adult-facing metrics;
- stop conditions and named operator;
- explicit approval timestamp and approver.

Any material edit invalidates the approval and returns the campaign to draft. A reusable channel playbook is not approval to spend.

## Pre-activation gate

Qualified review must cover current COPPA, applicable Massachusetts/state law, platform child-safety/advertising/developer/data-use rules, notices and consent, data transfers, and retention. This prompt and this repository are not legal approval.
