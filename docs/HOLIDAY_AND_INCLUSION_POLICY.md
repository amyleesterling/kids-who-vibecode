# Holiday, Culture, and Inclusion Policy

## Principle

Special-event challenges are optional invitations, never a test of whether a child celebrates, belongs, understands a tradition, or completes every week. A neutral seasonal or imaginative alternative appears beside the observance prompt with equal visual weight.

Do not turn sacred symbols, painful history, cultural clothing, food, flags, deities, languages, or rituals into collectibles, obstacles, costumes, power-ups, or random decoration. Do not combine distinct traditions into a generic lights-and-celebration package.

## Required evidence and approvals

Every bonus stores an authoritative date/context URL, date caveats, a neutral alternative, its review gate, and a noncompetitive status. Lunar New Year, Pride, Juneteenth, and Diwali/Deepavali require specialist inclusion/context review plus Amy’s recorded approval before scheduling. AI cannot supply cultural authority.

Date/context sources used for the 2027 draft set:

- [U.S. Office of Personnel Management 2027 federal holidays](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)
- [Hong Kong Observatory 2027 calendar conversion table](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/calendar/pdf/files/2027e.pdf)
- [UNESCO International Day of Mathematics](https://www.unesco.org/en/days/mathematics)
- [U.S. Naval Observatory seasons API](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/api/seasons?year=2027)
- [United Nations Creativity and Innovation Day](https://www.un.org/en/observances/creativity-and-innovation-day) and [Earth Day](https://www.un.org/en/observances/earth-day)
- [Library of Congress Pride Month context](https://www.loc.gov/lgbt-pride-month/about/)
- [National Park Service Juneteenth context](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/juneteenth.htm)
- [United Nations International Day of Friendship](https://www.un.org/en/observances/international-day-friendship)
- [Government of India 2027 holiday circular](https://ncr.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1785500162425-NCRPS%206464_2026_merged.pdf)

The government circular uses October 29 for Diwali and permits October 28 in states observing Naraka Chaturdasi. The draft preserves that disagreement instead of erasing it. Equinox/solstice timestamps are stored in UTC and explained locally; seasons are not assumed to be the same in both hemispheres.

## Review questions

1. Is the date and context supported by a primary or authoritative source?
2. Does the prompt distinguish the particular tradition from nearby traditions?
3. Can a child participate without affirming a belief or disclosing identity, heritage, or family practice?
4. Is the neutral alternative genuinely equal, not an afterthought?
5. Are facts curated rather than generated from memory?
6. Are painful histories treated as history rather than game material?
7. Does the visual brief avoid tokenism and unapproved sacred imagery?
8. Has the named human gate been recorded?

Any “no,” missing evidence, disagreement, or uncertainty keeps the item private and unscheduled.
