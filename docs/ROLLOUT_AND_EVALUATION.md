# Rollout and Evaluation

## Phase 1 — Offline evaluation

Run versioned policy against synthetic/adversarial fixtures and, only where authorized, de-identified historical cases. Preserve expected labels, actual results, limitations, policy/model/prompt versions, and environment. No publication path exists.

Exit requires migration/projection/permission tests, adversarial coverage, reproducible policy output, and no unresolved critical defect.

## Phase 2 — Shadow mode

Every new submission receives an auditable proposed green/yellow/red lane while existing grown-up moderation remains authoritative. Compare proposal, human action, misses, unnecessary escalations, disagreement types, and missing evidence. Record every comparison; do not train or profile children from individual behavior.

Current branch implements this state and constrains decisions to shadow. The legacy human action remains the only publisher.

## Phase 3 — Limited green-lane pilot

Not implemented or authorized. A future separate PR may propose only a reviewed immutable/controlled-origin static-artifact category after the predeclared 300-case/12-week/8-format threshold, zero critical false greens, 299/300 noncritical agreement, twice-clean benchmarks, staging kill-switch/takedown/rollback proof, qualified legal/privacy review, and Amy’s exact approval.

Every pilot auto-approval receives grown-up audit. One critical miss, target drift, or material policy/model/prompt change disables the pilot and resets the window.

## Phase 4 — Controlled operation

Not implemented or authorized. Future operation audits a random green sample, all policy changes, drift, and benchmark regressions. It preserves immediate takedown and automatically returns to shadow on a critical miss.

## Annual operating cadence

- Before each arc: curriculum/accessibility/inclusion review and schedule conflict check.
- Before each week: copy/link/date/alt-text review; website/content preview; parent email and social remain approval-bound.
- During submission: shadow evidence health, yellow/red queue, human turnaround, and scanner isolation/failure checks.
- After voting: favorites review, adult-facing package verification, aggregate metrics, and future-draft adjustment without rewriting history.
- On policy/model/prompt change: rerun the entire benchmark and record the new version before use.
- At session end: update `.endeavor/EVIDENCE.md`, `RISKS.md`, `RUN_LOG.md`, and `HANDOFF.md`.

## Metrics and interpretation

Measure aggregate participation, lane distribution/reasons, human-agent disagreement, turnaround, content-format diversity, seasonal participation, adult newsletter/social engagement, demo load success, and accessibility failures. Minimum group size defaults to 10. Empty/small cells stay private.

Metrics guide future drafts; they do not rank children, maximize screen time, preserve streaks, or define advertising audiences. Qualitative grown-up feedback and safety incidents can outweigh growth numbers.

## Closure levels

- Local source + tests: C2/E2–E3.
- Staging controls and integrated providers: C3/E4, future approval.
- Independent legal/cultural/safety validation: C4/E5 where required.
- Production auto-approval/live social/paid promotion: blocked until their named approvals; no local test can close that boundary.
